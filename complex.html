    Help & privacy

    Use the keys or your keyboard. Edit the expression to correct any part before pressing Enter or =. Select a history entry to edit and calculate it again. Enter calculates, Backspace deletes, Escape clears. Powers use ^. Functions act on the current number or the most recent parenthesized group. Percent divides that value by 100. Clear deletes one character; AC resets the expression. Memory persists until cleared or this page is closed.

    Trigonometry starts in degrees; select Deg/Rad to switch. History, theme and sound preferences are saved in this browser when storage is available. Toggle button sounds in Settings. Each calculator mode saves its own history. Delete this history removes only calculations saved in the current mode. Currency rates are fetched automatically from ExchangeRate-API and cached in this browser. All currencies supported by that provider are available. Rates update daily and may differ from your bank. Only the rate request is sent to the provider; your amounts and calculation history stay in this browser.
